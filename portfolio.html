7. Suggestions for Improvement

Customer Reviews
Encourage satisfied customers to leave genuine reviews and respond professionally to feedback.
Loyalty Program
Introduce simple loyalty rewards to encourage repeat visits and improve customer retention.
Social Media Engagement
Use polls, questions, reels and customer-generated content to encourage regular interaction.
Personalized Offers
Provide relevant offers for students, families, professionals and returning customers.
Local SEO
Keep the restaurant's Google Business Profile updated with photos, menu, offers, timings and reviews.
Customer Feedback
Collect customer feedback regularly and use it to improve food, service and overall dining experience.
